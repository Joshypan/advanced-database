A Transaction Is a with Block

with dataset.connect("sqlite:///pets.db") as tx:

Foreign Keys Are Off Until Asked

db = dataset.connect(
    "sqlite:///pets.db",
    on_connect_statements=["PRAGMA foreign_keys=ON"])

Without it: a pet with kind_id 999 goes in, and a kind in use can be deleted.
